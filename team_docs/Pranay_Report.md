# Individual Contribution Report: Pranay
**Role: UI/UX & Presentation Lead**

## 1. Role Overview
Pranay served as the creative director of the TrustRent project, ensuring that the platform not only functioned perfectly but looked and felt like a premium, state-of-the-art product. His role combined high-level visual design with complex frontend interaction logic. Beyond the code, Pranay was the lead architect of the project’s presentation strategy, crafting the "TrustRent story" that was delivered to stakeholders via the professional PPT.

## 2. Key Contributions
### A. Visual Identity & Design System
Pranay was the architect of the "TrustRent Look." He moved the project away from generic browser defaults and implemented a curated design system. He chose a sophisticated color palette—utilizing deep teals (`#0D7377`) as the primary brand color and vibrant ambers for accents—to create a "Trust-worthy" aesthetic. He implemented modern typography (Inter and Outfit) and utilized Glassmorphism effects (backdrop-blur) on headers and modals to give the platform a lightweight, modern feel. This visual excellence was a major highlight during the project demo.

### B. Dashboard UX & Interface Design
Pranay designed and implemented the dual-role dashboard system. He ensured that the **Tenant Dashboard** focused on discovery and booking tracking, while the **Landlord Dashboard** prioritized property management and lead responding. He utilized a "Mobile-First" responsive design approach, ensuring that the navigation bars, sidebar menus, and property grids collapsed elegantly on smaller screens. His use of subtle hover effects and micro-animations (using CSS transitions) made the interface feel "alive" and interactive.

### C. Real-time Messaging Frontend
One of the most praised features—the Messages Page—was designed and refined by Pranay. He implemented the complex split-view chat interface, managing the state transitions between the conversation list and the active chat window. He ensured that the messaging UI handled polling status gracefully, adding "Sender" and "Recipient" identity logic so users always knew who they were talking to. Following the final polish phase, he successfully implemented the "No-Scroll" logic to give users full control over their reading experience in long chat threads.

### D. Presentation & PPT Storyboarding (Lead Author)
Pranay took the lead on the Project Presentation (PPT). He translated the complex technical work into a compelling 15-slide narrative. He designed the PPT to reflect the app’s brand, using screenshots, feature highlights, and a "Problem vs. Solution" framework to demonstrate the platform’s value proposition. He coordinated with Madhura to include data-driven slides on market analysis and user testing results. His work on the PPT ensured that the project’s technical complexity was communicated clearly and impressively.

## 3. Methodology and Tools
Pranay’s methodology was "Aesthetics as a Feature." He believed that a beautiful app builds trust—a core value of the TrustRent brand. He used a "Rapid Prototyping" workflow, often building UI components in isolation before integrating them into the main React app. He focused heavily on the "Last 5% Policy"—spending extra time on polish items like custom loading spinners, error toasts, and empty-state illustrations that made the app feel finished.

**Tools Used:**
- **Design:** Figma, Canva (for PPT graphics)
- **Frontend Logic:** React, Tailwind CSS
- **Interactions:** CSS3 Animations, Framer Motion (basic)
- **Collaboration:** Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides
- **Icons:** Lucide React, FontAwesome

## 4. Impact
Pranay’s work was the "Face" of TrustRent. His dedication to visual excellence meant the project stood out from traditional student prototypes. The polished PPT he led was instrumental in securing high marks for the presentation phase, while his messaging UI and dashboard designs provided the smooth, "App-like" experience that users now enjoy.
